Great to welcome Prof. Johan Hofkens and Dr. Elke Debroye from KU Leuven for a pair of seminars on campus. Prof. Hofkens (KU Leuven and the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research) spoke on "Chemistry Driven Microscopy: from improved imaging to applications," and Dr. Elke Debroye (Associate Professor, KU Leuven) followed with "Shedding Light on Perovskites: Spectroscopic Insights into Stable Optoelectronics."
