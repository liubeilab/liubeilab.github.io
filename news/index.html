News

From the lab.

Trips, milestones, new instruments and the occasional hotpot.

  1. 22 August 2026

    Prof. Per Uhlén visits PKU

    Great to have Prof. Per Uhlén (Karolinska Institutet) visit the PKU campus for an afternoon of science with students — a round-table discussion around his talk, "Seeing is Believing: Exploring Whole-Brain RNA Transcripts in Three Dimensions."

  2. 18 June 2026

    On the Great Wall with Prof. Fernando D. Stefani

    The day after his seminar, the lab took Prof. Fernando D. Stefani out to the Great Wall for a day away from the microscopes.

  3. 17 June 2026

    Prof. Fernando D. Stefani visits NBIC

    Prof. Fernando D. Stefani — of CIBION–CONICET and the University of Buenos Aires — was invited to the National Biomedical Imaging Center, where he gave a talk titled "Fluorescence Nanoscopy and Single Molecule Tracking with True Nanometric Resolution: STED-FRET, MINFLUX and Other Ways."

  4. 16 May 2026

    A lab trip to Datong

    The lab headed to Datong, in Shanxi, for a weekend away — the old city walls, its temples and grottoes, and a group photo dressed up as the local paper's front page.

  5. 17 January 2026

    A lab dinner to welcome the Chinese New Year

    Ahead of the Spring Festival, the whole lab came together for a New Year dinner — a warm evening of good food and good company to close out the year and welcome the one ahead.

  6. 6 November 2025

    The 3rd Sino–France Joint Meeting on BioImaging

    The lab took part in the 3rd Sino–France Joint Meeting on BioImaging, held in Huairou, bringing together biomedical imaging groups from China and France.

  7. 3 November 2025

    At the French Embassy: 30 years of CNRS in China

    The lab joined a reception at the French Embassy in China celebrating 30 years of CNRS — France's national centre for scientific research — in China, together with the ambassador and visiting scientists from France.

  8. 26 June 2025

    A lab day at Universal Studios Beijing

    The students took a day off together for a trip to Universal Studios Beijing.

  9. 23 May 2025

    Installing our Leica SP8 FLIM in Huairou

    Our Leica SP8 FLIM confocal microscope being installed at the Huairou lab — another instrument online.

  10. 23 April 2025

    Onur Dagliyan visits Beijing

    So good to have our friend Onur Dagliyan in Beijing — in town for the Huairou Forum, and around for dinner and good conversation.

  11. 21 March 2025

    Our imaging facility passes national acceptance

    Celebrating the national acceptance of the Multi-modal Cross-scale Biomedical Imaging Facility, in Huairou — the milestone that carries our work, and one that even featured on the national evening news. We are officially setting sail.

  12. 16 December 2024

    Presenting at ChemoRevolution

    The lab presented at ChemoRevolution — "Chem Revolution: From Enabling Tools to Enlightening Discoveries" — with an oral talk from Bei Liu and posters from the students.

  13. 21 May 2023

    Advanced Imaging for Cell Discovery, Hefei

    The lab travelled to Hefei, Anhui for the Advanced Imaging for Cell Discovery meeting.

  14. 14 February 2023

    Catching the tail end of winter — Huaibei Ski

    To celebrate the beginning of the coming semester, the Liu lab went skiing and then HOTPOT afterward, together with other FOUR labs! Much fun. :))

  15. 7 January 2023

    Happy New Year

    We celebrated the New Year together with the Sun lab (Sun He). Looking forward to more collaborations ahead — and hot pot is always the students' first choice.

  16. 31 December 2022

    Our air table arrived before New Year's Day

    Our optical air table arrived just before New Year's Day — the groundwork for the microscopes to come.

  17. 5 November 2022

    A lab outing to the Great Wall and Hongluo Temple

    An early lab outing — a day at the Mutianyu Great Wall and Hongluo Temple.

  18. 4 November 2022

    At the NBIC completion ceremony and the 2nd Huairou Forum

    The lab took part in the completion ceremony of the National Biomedical Imaging Center and the 2nd Huairou Forum.

  19. 7 August 2022

    The Liu Lab is founded

    The Liu Lab officially began on 8 August 2022. We set out to build next-generation imaging and genetic tools to understand the mysteries of life.
